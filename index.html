Ross Impact Resume Maker

Open-sourced by Nick Furton

Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Michigan or the Ross School of Business. Your resume stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

The page layout matches the Ross resume builder: same margins, type sizes, line width, and spacing, measured from real output. The built-in font is Carlito, an open font with the same letter widths as Calibri, so line breaks never change. Calibri itself cannot be hosted on a website, but the app can use the Calibri already installed on your computer and embed it in your PDF.
